The ledger rule

All twenty recorded decisions are still open. The ADR index states that every decision is proposed until it is explicitly accepted, and that numbers are preserved: later clarifications get new numbers instead of renumbering earlier ones (docs/adr/README.md:3). The repo's agent guidance adds that ADR status stays as it is until the official Notion ledger records a decision (AGENTS.md:3). No page here is marked accepted, and none should be until that ledger says so.
